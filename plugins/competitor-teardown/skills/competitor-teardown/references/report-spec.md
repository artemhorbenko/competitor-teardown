# Report specification

One `audit.json` feeds two outputs:
1. **The report page** (`assets/report-template.html` via `scripts/build_report.py`) for people.
2. **The hands-off file** (`scripts/build_handoff.py` → `handoff.md` + `tasks.json`) for an AI agent or developer who implements the moves.

You write only the JSON (`audit.schema.json`, example `assets/example-teardown.en.json`). Rebuild the page from this spec only if the template cannot be used.

## Look

Annual-report style, the same family as product-lens-audit: white ground, black ink, one vermilion accent (#ff4a1c) for "us", the criteria where we trail and the top 3 moves; amber for partial; dark theme on #120d0b (follows `prefers-color-scheme` and a `data-theme` attribute). Titles in Sofia Sans Condensed 800, text in Sofia Sans (Latin and Cyrillic). Stroke SVG icons per lens and per move area, no emoji. Works at 360px: tables become stacked cards. Every derived number (lead and trail counts, ICE, quick wins, confidence) is computed by the template.

Headings state their point ("Kinship owns the comparison; we have no answer"), not a topic. If any data is sample, say so once in `text.label`. Footer: generated by competitor-teardown.

## Sections, in order

1. **Header**: headline = the main gap, with its number; one-sentence lead; up to 4 KPIs (typically `{trail}/{crit}` criteria where we trail, our best review number, one market fact, quick wins); a products strip with each product's line style (us in vermilion), one-line tag and domain.
2. **Comparison matrix**: products × criteria grouped by lens. Each cell: ✓ / ◐ / ✗ / – (no data), a short note, a numbered source link. Our column is tinted; a red bar marks rows where we trail the best competitor. Chips: all / only differences / where we trail. One vendor's claim about another shows "(per Vendor)".
3. **Strategy canvas**: a line per product across 5-10 factors (0-10); us in vermilion, competitors in ink and grey with dash patterns and marker shapes; end labels on wide screens, a legend always, numbered factors with a key on narrow screens.
4. **ERRC grid** (eliminate, reduce, raise, create) beside the **positioning map** (two labelled axes, points, quadrant guides).
5. **Where we win / where we lose**: two columns of short points, each with sources and the competitors involved.
6. **Impact-effort matrix** of moves (filled = optimize, outlined = explore, top 3 vermilion, quick-wins quadrant tinted).
7. **Moves table**: sorted by ICE, chips all / optimize / explore; columns #, move (observed/hypothesis badge), area, answers (competitors), type, I, C, E, ICE.
8. **Next 3 moves**: cards with hypothesis, success metric, cheapest test.
9. **Full plan**: one collapsible row per move, top 3 open: why, competitor evidence (with source numbers), metric, effort, risk; for the top 3 also alternatives, risk type, threshold, pre-mortem and copy variants.
10. **Sources and confidence**: confidence meter, start answers, the numbered source list (name linked to URL, status, kind, checked-on date, note), glossary, how the report was made.

## Data shapes that are easy to get wrong

- `products`: 2-6, exactly one `us: true`. Ids are short slugs used everywhere else.
- `criteria[].cells` has an entry for **every** product id. `nd` needs no source; every other value needs `t` and `s`.
- `sources[].id` is referenced by cells, evidence, win/lose and KPIs. A source with a `url` needs `date`. `kind` `own`/`rival` needs `by`. A cell about product P citing a page by another vendor must have `claim: true`.
- `canvas.factors[].levels` has every product id (0-10) and `basis` lists criterion ids.
- `map.points` has every product id (0-10 on both axes).
- `errc` has all four keys; items may point to a move with `op` (1-based).
- `ops[].a` is one of `page`, `message`, `pricing`, `feature`, `content`, `proof`, `sales`, `data`; `vs` lists competitor ids.
- `detail[id].evidence` is a non-empty list of `{p, t, s, claim?}`.
- `text` tokens: `{lead}` `{trail}` `{crit}` `{cells}` `{qw}` `{n}` `{conf}`; `[[words]]` turn red. All text is escaped.
- Quotes (`"..."`, `“...”`, `«...»`) are at most 15 words anywhere in the file.

## Tooltips

Every abbreviation and framework name (ICE, ERRC, Blue Ocean, strategy canvas, MCP, API, G2, Tranco, pre-mortem...) is a dotted-underlined button with a tooltip from `glossary`: hover on desktop; tap on phones and tablets, second tap or a tap elsewhere closes; keyboard focus opens, Escape closes; scrolling 40px closes. The script wraps the first occurrence per paragraph or cell.

## Phone tables

In both stacked tables each cell's content is wrapped in exactly one `<span class="cv">`, again after every filter re-render, and each cell carries `data-l` (product name or column name) for its label. Without the wrapper the text and tooltip buttons break into columns.

## Robustness

- Charts are drawn at real pixel size with a ResizeObserver; labels are clamped inside the chart and spread so they do not overlap.
- All script sits in one block at the end of `<body>`, wrapped in `{ }`; check it with `node --check`.
- No `localStorage`.
